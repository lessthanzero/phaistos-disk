# Question-Driven Research Agenda

Each research question represents an empirical investigation to be addressed through reproducible experiments.

* **Q001**: How statistically unusual is the Disc's sign frequency distribution compared to natural languages and non-linguistic symbol sets?
* **Q002**: How unusual are repeated sign groups (e.g. `02-12-31-26`, `02-27-25-10-23-18`), and do they represent refrains, morphological affixes, or ritual repetitions?
* **Q003**: Does sign position (initial, medial, final) exhibit the positional entropy of a syllabary, alphabet, or logographic system?
* **Q004**: Does the Disc exhibit measurable language-like informational entropy and compressibility?
* **Q005**: Do proposed Linear A / Linear B correspondences outperform chance matches against randomized control corpora?
* **Q006**: Does a syllabic CV / CVC model fit the corpus better than chance?
* **Q007**: Do candidate Bronze Age languages (Minoan substrate, Luwian, Mycenaean Greek, Semitic) outperform random control models?
* **Q008**: Which published decipherments make verifiable falsifiable predictions?
* **Q009**: Does any proposed decipherment generalize to held-out groups or the Arkalochori Axe?
* **Q010**: Does a non-linguistic model (lunisolar calendar, game board rules, geometric mnemonic) explain the data with fewer ad-hoc assumptions?
* **Q011**: Which assumptions do most of the explanatory work in each hypothesis?
* **Q012**: Can independent computational methods converge on the same sign values without shared priors?
